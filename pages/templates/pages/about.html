The short version

{{ company.name }} is led by {{ company.founder }}, an architectural designer who has been in practice since 2012. He holds a Diploma in Technology (Architecture) from The Technical University of Kenya, and has spent the years since working across private residences, resort and hospitality developments, and institutional buildings.

His portfolio covers nine disclosed projects with a combined value of around KES 103 million, in counties from Nairobi and Kajiado to Kakamega, Bungoma, Siaya, Baringo, Machakos, Kisumu and Uasin Gishu. The largest is a maternity wing commissioned by the M-Pesa Foundation at Likuyani in Kakamega County.

Alongside client work, he mentors students on industrial attachment, giving them practical exposure to both design and site management.

How we work

We start with your budget

A design you cannot afford to build is a waste of everyone's time. We test the budget as the design develops, not after.

You see it before it is built

Designs are presented in 3D so you are approving a building you can actually picture, not a floor plan you have to decode.

We stay on site

Drawings only protect you if somebody checks the work against them. We inspect at every stage, from setting out to finishes.

One point of contact

You deal with the person doing the work. Nothing is handed off to someone you have never met.

A note on scope

We provide architectural design, design-and-build and construction supervision services. Where a project requires a registered architect or engineer to sign off statutory submissions, we work alongside the appropriate registered professional. Several projects in our portfolio were delivered with Eco Space Architects, and each project page states the role held.
